## Why this exists

Most SEO teaching is a list of things that are true. Fix your titles. Improve your Core
Web Vitals. Build links. All correct, all useless on their own, because the thing that
decides whether a site grows is not *which* of those you do — it is which one you do
**first**, on this site, this quarter.

That judgement is the whole job, and it is the part that almost nobody teaches. It is
what this bootcamp is.

## How it is taught

Every module follows the same shape: here is the decision you are trying to make, here is
how I make it, here is me making it on a real site, here is the exercise on yours. You are
not watching a tool demo. You are watching someone decide, and being told why.

The templates are the ones I actually use — the audit sheet, the keyword map, the brief,
the reporting script. Not cleaned-up teaching versions. The real ones, with the awkward
bits still in them.

## Who is teaching it

I run SEO, digital marketing and AI automation for businesses across Europe, from
Pristina. I spend most of my week doing this work on client sites rather than writing
about it, which is the only reason the course is worth anything — it is a record of what
currently works, not a summary of what has been published about what used to.
