## Why this exists

WordPress makes it easy to create a page. That is not the same as making it easy to
build a good website. The difficult part is knowing what to install, what to leave out,
how to structure the pages and how to launch without discovering that the site is slow,
fragile or impossible to maintain.

This bootcamp teaches the complete build, in order. You begin with a blank plan and end
with a live website you understand — not a template held together by plugins you are
afraid to update.

## How it is taught

Every module moves the same website closer to launch. I explain the decision, make it on
the site, show the common mistakes, and then give you the matching task on your own build.
The checklists and templates are designed to be reused on the next website as well.

The goal is not to memorise where WordPress keeps every setting. It is to learn a process
you can repeat even when the theme, host or client changes.

## Who is teaching it

I build WordPress websites from scratch and work across the parts that decide whether
they succeed after launch: structure, design, performance, SEO, security and ongoing
maintenance. This course packages that practical workflow so you can build independently
and know when a shortcut will cost more later.
