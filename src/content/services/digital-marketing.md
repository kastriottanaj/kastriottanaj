## What this is

The work between "people can find us" and "people become customers." Visibility on the
channels that matter for your business, campaigns with a point to them, and a conversion
path that does not lose people at the last step.

## How I work on it

The starting question is always where your customers already spend attention, and what
they need to see before they will get in touch. That determines the channel mix — not
what happens to be fashionable.

Then it is execution and measurement. Campaigns get planned with a defined goal, run,
and reported against that goal. If a channel is not producing after a fair run, we stop
doing it rather than quietly continuing.

## Who it suits

Businesses that have something worth talking about and no consistent system for talking
about it — or ones running activity across several channels without a clear read on which
of them is working.
